Our approach

I follow the work from the first step to the next.

Where does an inquiry arrive? Who responds? What happens after an appointment? How does a document get signed? What does the next person need to keep things moving?

These connections matter. I look closely at how work passes between people and tools, then help create a clearer, more connected process.

Lender Matching
Part of LenderNest

Explore lender options with organized information.

Enter the scenario once. AI-enhanced matching reviews the lenders in your library and shows which programs fit, how they price and why.

  • AI-enhanced matching against a researched lender library, with every program and link
  • Customized to your lenders, programs and guidelines
  • Live pricing from rate sheets that replace themselves when lenders send new ones
  • A nightly email sweep that adds new lenders, new contacts and new rate sheets
  • LTV, DSCR, LTC and payment as you type, even with a negative NOI in lease-up
  • Every property type, from rentals and mixed-use to senior care facilities
  • State eligibility checked before anything is quoted
  • One-click scenario emails to the lenders you pick, with replies going straight to the loan officer
  • Every scenario logged on the file, showing who it went to and when
  • The same pricer on the dashboard and inside every loan file
  • Term sheets compared side by side, with AI explaining the pros and cons
Lender research that once meant hours of shopping becomes one organized search.
