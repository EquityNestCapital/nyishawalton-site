A lending operating system

LenderNest

Ome Sanchez and I spent years watching good loan files slow down because the work lived in too many places. LenderNest is the system we wished we had: lender matching, pricing, a CRM, processing, HR and financial tracking, all in one home.

The first morning I worked seven loan files before lunch, I knew it was real. It gave us time back, and that is what we want to build for every business owner we work with.

Designed by Nyisha Walton and Ome Sanchez

A day trading education platform

The Greenprint

Jacob built The Greenprint for traders who learn best by watching it happen. Live sessions, trade signals and member chat sit side by side, with an options scanner and a playbook close at hand.

Behind the scenes, a student CRM and automations keep every member moving, so the teaching stays front and center.

Designed by Jacob Walton

Visit The Greenprint

A document signing platform

Carta Ink

We were paying every month to send documents for signature, so we built our own. Carta Ink sends documents with plain-language summaries, seals every signed copy with a certificate, and keeps everything in an archive.

It replaced a subscription in our own businesses, and it is built to do the same for yours.

Designed by Nyisha Walton
