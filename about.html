About

Nyisha Walton

Mortgage leader, loan officer trainer and builder of the systems that run real lending businesses.

Licensed loan officer and branch manager, and CEO and one of the three founders of EquityNest Capital.

In the mortgage industry

A lifetime of closings, built with a team.

Since 2015, Nyisha and her teams have helped more than 1,000 families. As a front-end producer she personally closed more than 500 loans, and the teams she has led have closed hundreds more. She once led a team of 50 loan officers, and in 2022, her record year, she originated 227 loans. She is the first to say none of it happened alone.

Today she leads EquityNest Capital with her co-founders. EquityNest arranges business purpose financing for real estate investors and business owners in over 30 states: DSCR rental loans, fix and flip, landlord and portfolio loans, commercial real estate, cannabis industry financing and business lines of credit.

Her licensed home loan work is done through Your Mortgage Plug.

Technology

She builds the systems lenders run on.

LenderNest is the commercial loan origination system and CRM she helped build for investor lending: applications, pricing and matching, processing, conditions, documents and the team behind every file, in one place.

She also builds custom CRMs, operating systems and websites for businesses that are ready to grow, including The Real Estate Rolodex, a directory of real estate professionals in all 50 states, and Carta Ink, a document e-signing company used across the United States.

Training

Helping loan officers grow.

Nyisha trains loan officers in sales, lead generation and the daily habits that build a book of business. She helps them expand into investor and commercial lending, use social media to bring in leads, and put systems in place so they can scale without burning out.

For business owners, she helps connect how the business looks, how it communicates and how it operates, so it can grow with less effort.
