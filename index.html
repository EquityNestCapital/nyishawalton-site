Built from firsthand experience

I helped build LenderNest, and it changed my life.

One of my greatest accomplishments has been building LenderNest with my business partner, Ome Sanchez. Together we designed a complete loan operating system shaped around the lending work we both know deeply.

One morning, I worked through seven loan files before lunchtime. That included pricing scenarios, matching lenders, obtaining terms, and coordinating outreach to clients and account executives.

Seeing those connections work together felt like magic. The system gave me more capacity to focus on the work that needed my expertise, and more time for my life.

That experience shapes what we build for other business owners. We study how the work moves, connect the steps, and create tools that support the people doing it.
